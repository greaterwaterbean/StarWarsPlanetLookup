How to use Planet Lookup

Moving around

  • Drag to spin the planet (right-drag works in every tool)
  • Scroll or pinch to zoom toward the cursor
  • Double-click to fly in
  • Arrow keys rotate, + - zoom, R resets

Places

  • L then click the globe to add a city or key location
  • Click a place to edit it. Drag it to move it
  • Mark a place Secret to hide it in player view

Painting

  • B paint biomes, raise or lower land, or paint regions
  • Pick a planet type and settings in the Planet tab first, then paint the details

Shortcuts

  • V select, M measure, Esc cancel
  • Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo
  • G cell grid, C clouds, X pixel size
  • P player view, Del delete selected place

Your work saves automatically in this browser. Use the Data tab to export a backup file.
